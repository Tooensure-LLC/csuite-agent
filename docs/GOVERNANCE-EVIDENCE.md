# Governance evidence

PMCR-O treats Microsoft Agent Governance Toolkit (AGT) or another registered governance provider as the source of truth for policy/identity/audit records. The queue carries references such as audit record ID, evidence artefact digest/URI, policy ID, intervention point, and verification pointers.

The business product can correlate those records with PMCR-O decisions and outcomes without turning the PMCR-O trail into a duplicate governance database.
